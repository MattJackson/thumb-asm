LicenseRef-Arm-Documentation
============================

This is not a licence grant. It is a statement of what certain files in this
repository are, so that an automated scanner and a human reader both get a
true answer instead of a convenient one.

WHAT IT COVERS

  spec/ARMv5_DDI0100I.pdf      ARM Architecture Reference Manual, DDI 0100I
  spec/ARMv7-M_DDI0403E.pdf    Armv7-M Architecture Reference Manual, DDI 0403E.e
  spec/ARMv7-AR_DDI0406C.pdf   ARMv7-A/R Architecture Reference Manual, DDI 0406C
  spec/ARMv6-M_DDI0419.pdf     Armv6-M Architecture Reference Manual, DDI 0419C

  spec/ARMv7-M.txt             plain-text dumps of the above, produced with
  spec/ARMv7-AR.txt            pypdf so that the encodings can be grepped
  spec/ARMv6-M.txt

WHY THEY ARE HERE

  thumb-asm encodes and decodes Arm Thumb instructions. Every encoding claim it
  makes is a claim about what these documents say, and a claim like that is only
  worth anything if the reader can check it. They are kept in the tree, next to
  the code, so that checking is a grep rather than a download — see spec/README.md
  for the section numbers and the retrieval provenance of each file.

  They are reference material for developing and reviewing this crate. They are
  not a component of it: Cargo.toml's `exclude` keeps spec/ out of the published
  .crate (enforced by the `package` job in .github/workflows/qa.yml), so no
  Arm document is redistributed to anyone installing thumb-asm from crates.io.

  Get them from Arm, who publish them for free after a click-through:

    https://developer.arm.com/documentation/ddi0100/latest/   (DDI 0100)

    https://developer.arm.com/documentation/ddi0406/latest/   (DDI 0406)
    https://developer.arm.com/documentation/ddi0419/latest/   (DDI 0419)

  Arm's own terms govern what you may then do with them. If Arm would prefer
  these copies not be mirrored here, they should be removed from the repository:
  open an issue at https://github.com/MattJackson/thumb-asm/issues and they will
  be, and spec/README.md's provenance table plus the section numbers cited
  throughout the source are enough to keep every claim checkable without them.
